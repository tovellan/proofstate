# Code of conduct

Project spaces are for constructive technical collaboration.

Participants must be respectful, specific, and considerate. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of private
information, and sustained disruption are not accepted.

Technical disagreement is expected. Address the design or evidence, not the
person. Give maintainers time to investigate reports and avoid publishing
sensitive details before a coordinated disclosure.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban an
account when needed to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should be
proportionate, documented privately, and reviewable by another maintainer when
one is available.

Report conduct concerns through a private GitHub security advisory and label the
report as a conduct concern. Do not include unnecessary personal information.
